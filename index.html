04 / The method

Strategy,
then pixels.

A seven-stage workflow blending human judgment with AI-assisted research, design systems, frontend development, and continuous refinement.

01

Discovery

Understanding goals, audience, positioning, constraints, and the actual problem behind the request.

02

AI Research

Using AI for ideation, trends, competitive analysis, content exploration, and faster research loops.

03

Wireframing

Planning structure, information architecture, user flows, hierarchy, and conversion paths before visual polish.

04

UI Design

Building a coherent visual language with typography, spacing, color, components, interaction patterns, and brand identity.

05

Development

Turning the system into responsive, performant, maintainable frontend code with intentional interaction.

06

Optimization

Improving performance, accessibility, SEO, responsiveness, and the details that make the experience feel finished.

07

Launch

Delivering a complete digital product ready to be used, measured, improved, and connected to real business goals.
